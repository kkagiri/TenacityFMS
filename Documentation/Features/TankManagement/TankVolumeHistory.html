TankVolumeHistoryIntegrationService

This service is a crucial component of the Tank Management feature. It provides a centralized way to process all changes that affect tank volume, ensuring that the `TankVolumeHistory` is always consistent and up-to-date. It is called by various commands, such as `CreateDeliveryCommand`, `CreateFuelRefillCommand`, and `CreateTankTransfer`.

Methods

  • ProcessFuelRefillChangeAsync: Processes a fuel refill and updates the `TankVolumeHistory`.
  • ProcessDeliveryChangeAsync: Processes a delivery and updates the `TankVolumeHistory`.
  • ProcessTankStockChangeAsync: Processes a tank stock change (opening or closing) and updates the `TankVolumeHistory`.
  • ProcessTankTransferOutChangeAsync: Processes the outgoing side of a tank transfer.
  • ProcessTankTransferInChangeAsync: Processes the incoming side of a tank transfer.
  • ProcessAdjustmentChangeAsync: Processes a manual stock adjustment.
  • ProcessPumpTransactionChangeAsync: Processes an automated pump transaction.
  • ReconcileTankCurrentStockAsync: Reconciles the `CurrentStock` of a tank with its `TankVolumeHistory`.

UpdateTankVolumeHistoryCommand

This command is responsible for recalculating the `NewVolume` for all `TankVolumeHistory` records for a given tank from a specified effective date. This is essential for maintaining data integrity when a historical transaction is added or modified.
