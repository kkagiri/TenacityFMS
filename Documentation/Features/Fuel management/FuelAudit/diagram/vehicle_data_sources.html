📈 Full Tank Vehicle Confidence Scoring

Confidence is based on how recently the vehicle was refueled relative to the audit period dates.

Days Since Last Refuel Confidence Level Estimation Accuracy Recommendation
0-3 days HIGH (>80%) Dead stock estimate is reliable Include in reconciliation
4-7 days MEDIUM (50-80%) Estimate may drift from actual Include with caution
8-14 days LOW (30-50%) Significant uncertainty Flag for review
15+ days VERY LOW (<30%) Dead stock highly uncertain Exclude or manual check
💡 Best Practice
For critical audits, consider forcing pickup refuels on audit dates to eliminate estimation uncertainty. This ensures all full-tank vehicles have 100% confidence on opening and closing positions.
